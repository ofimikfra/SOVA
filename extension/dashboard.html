How to use SOVA

1
Join a video call
2
Start SOVA Click Start SOVA or press Space.
3
Listen to descriptions Descriptions appear after your specified analysis interval. The latest one appears under Latest Description. All previous ones are under Description Feed below it, colour-coded by sentiment.
4
Adjust settings Go to Settings to change the analysis interval, TTS voice and volume, language, and the local AI model. Hit Save Settings or press Ctrl+S to apply.
5
Stop SOVA Click Stop SOVA or press Space again.
